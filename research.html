Zhiyu Wang

Research

Redox-active molecules and mediators designed for practical energy storage, conversion, recovery, and heat-harvesting systems.

Research focus

Molecular design for practical electrochemical systems.

My work follows redox-active molecules and mediators from structure design into devices that can store energy, recover battery materials, move hydrogen, and harvest small temperature differences.

01

Redox-mediated flow systems

Aqueous redox flow batteries and redox-targeting-based flow cells for energy and hydrogen storage, with emphasis on capacity recovery, proton-coupled electron transfer, and scalable system architectures.

02

Redox-active materials

Design and evaluation of zwitterionic azo posolytes, phenazine polymers, and hexaazatrinaphthalene derivatives for long-life electrochemical systems.

03

Energy conversion and catalysis

Redox-mediated hydrogen production and storage, electrosynthesis, battery-material recovery, and electrochemical harvesting of low-grade heat for power generation.

Current workbench

Research that moves between molecule, electrolyte, and device.

The through-line is system-level usefulness: mediators should do more than shuttle electrons. They should open new operating windows, recover value from spent materials, and make electrochemical processes easier to scale.
